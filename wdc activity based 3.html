🎒 Equipment Checklist

  • Sturdy, waterproof hiking boots
  • All-weather thermal layers & jacket
  • Sleeping bag (rated for sub-zero temperatures)
  • Trekking poles and durable backpack
  • First-aid kit and personal medications

⚠️ Safety Guidelines

  • Always follow the instructions of the lead mountain guide.
  • Stay hydrated and carry at least 2 liters of water daily.
  • Never venture off the marked trail alone.
  • Report any health discomfort or altitude symptoms immediately.
